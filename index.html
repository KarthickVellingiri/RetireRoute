Monthly simulation convention: each month applies nominal annual rate ÷ 12 to every asset and loan, then allocates that month’s cash flow. SIP and bonus contributions go pro-rata only to the 12% risk assets.

Clear First assumption: contractual EMIs are paid on every active loan, then the stepped-up SIP, annual bonus, and every EMI freed by paid-off loans are immediately recycled to the highest-rate remaining loan (avalanche).

This is an illustrative projection. Returns, inflation, taxes, fees and changes in income can materially affect actual results.
